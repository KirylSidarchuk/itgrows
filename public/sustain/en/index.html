Questions

Frequently asked questions

How much does a website cost?

Our packages start at €14,900 excl. VAT. For apps, platforms and bigger projects we make a custom quote.

How soon is my website live?

In 4 to 8 weeks from the intro call, depending on the package.

Who owns the website?

You do. The domain, the content and all logins stay with you.

Is the website GDPR compliant?

Yes. Legal pages, a privacy policy and cookie consent come with every package. On request we host in the EU.

What does visibility in ChatGPT mean?

More and more customers ask AI assistants instead of a search engine. We make sure these assistants can read your website and find enough about your business to name it.

Is the ad budget included?

No. You pay the budget for Google and Meta directly to the platforms. Setting up and running the campaigns is part of the package.

Do you only work in Germany?

We work with clients in Germany and internationally. Your website can be multilingual.
